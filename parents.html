For parents

Your player asked you to start Elite Hockey Drills

It is an off-ice training app for hockey players: strength, speed and conditioning, planned around their practices and games. It was built by Coach David Ciboch, M.Ed. Sport Science and former UAE national-team coach.

What your player gets

  • A program for their age, position and schedule: 2 to 5 sessions a week around practices and games, with lighter weeks built in.
  • A demo video for every exercise (390+), so they learn to do it right.
  • Training that fits their age. Players 14 and under use bodyweight and light equipment only, never barbells. After a concussion that is not yet cleared, hard landings, heavy spinal loading and all-out sprints stay out of the plan until a professional clears it.
  • Tests and personal records, so you can both see what is changing.

How to start it (2 minutes, on their phone)

  1. Sit down with your player and open the app on their phone. Their program is already saved.
  2. They tap the start button with the yearly plan selected.
  3. The App Store or Google Play asks for the payment. If your family uses purchase approval (Apple’s Ask to Buy in Family Sharing, or Google Family Link), you get a request on your own phone: approve it. Otherwise you confirm with the payment method on their store account.
  4. Done. The free week starts and the first workout is one tap away.

How to cancel

Cancel before day 7 and nothing is charged. Cancel on the account that holds the subscription, usually your player’s:

iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Elite Hockey Drills Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

After the free week, access runs to the end of the paid period.
