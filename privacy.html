Privacy Policy.

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BELLOSAURUS FOR ONLINE SELLING, a sole establishment licensed in the Emirate of Dubai, United Arab Emirates (commercial licence no. 1629119) (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information in connection with the Elite Hockey Drills mobile application (the “App”).

If you have any questions, contact us at coach@elitehockeydrills.com.

The short version

  • The App is built to work on your device. Your training profile, program, baselines, and workout history are stored locally on your phone, not on our servers.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.
  • You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Reset App & Delete All Data.
  • The App has a Youth mode for younger players; for children under 13 we require verifiable parental/guardian consent before any setup.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you provide during setup and use

To build and adjust your training program, the App asks you for information such as:

  • Optional first name or nickname
  • Age / date-of-birth bracket, and (for the Youth flow) guardian-consent status
  • Body metrics you choose to enter (e.g. height, weight)
  • Hockey background, position, goals, available equipment, and training schedule
  • Self-assessments, baseline test results, workout logs, and game logs you record

This information is stored locally on your device. We do not receive a copy of it unless and until you choose to use a cloud feature described below.

b) Optional email address

If you choose to provide an email address, it is currently stored on your device. When optional cloud backup launches (see “Cloud backup” below), your email will be used to back up and restore your program. Providing an email is always optional and the App works fully without one. We do not collect email addresses from users under 13.

c) Cloud backup and account data — when enabled

We plan to offer an optional account so your program can be backed up and restored across devices. When that feature is enabled, and only if you opt in, we will store your email and a copy of your profile/program with our cloud provider, Supabase. Until then, no such data leaves your device.

d) Usage and diagnostic data

To fix crashes and improve the App, we use PostHog (product analytics) and Sentry (crash reporting) to collect limited, largely de-identified information such as app events (e.g. screens viewed, features used), device model, OS version, and crash reports. You can turn this off at any time in the App under Settings → Share Anonymous Diagnostics. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

e) Purchase information

If you buy a subscription or other in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple or Google (and our purchase manager, RevenueCat). We receive your purchase/subscription status (e.g. “active”), not your full payment-card details.

f) Notifications

The App uses local notifications (reminders scheduled on your device) for things like workout and game-day prompts. We ask your permission first, and you can turn them off in Settings or your OS. We do not operate a push-notification server, and notifications do not send your data to us.

g) Permissions we do not use

The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or health/fitness platforms.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Generate and continually adjust your personalized training program
  • Show your progress, baselines, streaks, and history
  • Schedule the local reminders you opt into
  • Process and validate in-app purchases
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Legal bases (UAE PDPL / GDPR where applicable)

Where required, we rely on: your consent (e.g. optional email, notifications, analytics, children’s data); performance of a contract (providing the App and any purchases); and our legitimate interests (keeping the App secure and working). You can withdraw consent at any time (see “Your choices”).

4. Children’s privacy

The App offers a Youth mode designed for younger players, with age-appropriate programming.

  • For users under 13, the App requires verifiable parental or guardian consent before completing setup.
  • We collect only the limited information needed to provide age-appropriate training, and (today) it is stored on the child’s device. We do not collect email addresses from children under 13, and their data is never synced to the cloud.
  • We do not sell children’s data, show behavioral advertising, or knowingly collect more than is necessary.
  • A parent or guardian can review the information by opening the App on the device and can delete all of it via Settings → Reset App & Delete All Data, or by contacting us at coach@elitehockeydrills.com.

This is intended to align with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple’s and Google’s kids/family policies, and UAE data-protection requirements. If you believe a child has provided information without the required consent, contact us and we will delete it.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With Apple / Google / RevenueCat to process in-app purchases
  • With PostHog / Sentry for analytics and crash diagnostics
  • With Supabase to provide optional cloud backup (when enabled)
  • When required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security
  • In connection with a business transfer (e.g. merger or acquisition), subject to this Policy

We do not share your information with advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Data storage, security, and retention

  • Your training data is stored on your device and remains there until you delete it. Removing the App from your device deletes this local data.
  • Any cloud data (when that feature is enabled) is stored with Supabase and protected in transit using industry-standard encryption.
  • We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You can delete your data at any time (see below).

7. International transfers

The App is operated from the United Arab Emirates. If a cloud or service provider processes data outside the UAE, we take steps to ensure an appropriate level of protection consistent with applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your information, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because your data is stored on your device, you can exercise most of these directly:

  • Edit your details in the App’s profile/settings screens
  • Delete everything via Settings → Reset App & Delete All Data
  • Turn off notifications and anonymous diagnostics in Settings

To make any other request, email coach@elitehockeydrills.com. We will respond as required by applicable law (including the UAE PDPL and, where it applies, the GDPR/CCPA).

9. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third parties; their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play (distribution, purchases)
  • Expo (app build and update tooling)
  • RevenueCat (purchase management)
  • PostHog (product analytics), Sentry (crash reporting)
  • Supabase (optional cloud backup) — when enabled

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the App where appropriate.
